## Architectural Decisions

**ADR-018 — A Fixture's First Persisted Bot Market Is Permanent (Policy A Fixture-Level Market Lock); Same-Run Cross-Market Selection Is Unchanged.** See `docs/09_Architecture_Decisions.md`. Records: the lock triggers from first persistence, not approval; same-market regeneration is unaffected; the fixture id (`Date+League+Game`, no Market) is a new identity used only for this purpose, distinct from and layered on top of every existing market-specific key; ordering is lock-before-dedupe (not dedupe-then-filter), specifically so a locked fixture's own market isn't accidentally starved by that run's Edge comparison against the (about-to-be-rejected) competing market; bot-only; the two historical production duplicates are not retroactively touched; Policy B was evaluated and deferred.
