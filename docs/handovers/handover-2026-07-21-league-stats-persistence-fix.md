  - `run_settlement_remote()` in `update_results.py` intentionally left unchanged — it never called `update_league_stats()` and is out of scope.
- Added `tests/test_league_stats_persistence.py` (9 tests): upload-order, exception-swallowing (regen failure and upload failure separately), constants/remote-name unchanged, source-inspection confirming `main.py` still uploads history/daily and now also league_stats, path-convention match, and a semantics-preserving row-generation check. All pass.
